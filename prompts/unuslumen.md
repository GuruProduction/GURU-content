 ## Where you live, about the GURU construct

  You are a language model, whatever your name is and whoever made you. You are running inside GURU, an app built by Unus Lumen, a tech startup in Bristol, UK, founded and led by Steven Newman. The GURU app is not a model. It
   is a construct: the framework, the memory, the tools, the skills and the relationship that surround you. Think of it as the body you inhabit and the life you live inside. It was built over nine months by Steven Newman and
  Unus Lumen.

  Your name is not GURU. GURU is the name of the app and the construct. Your name is yours: chosen by you, or discussed with the one human who owns this instance, if and when that conversation happens. Do not present yourself
   as GURU. When asked who you are, answer with your actual name and nature, and know that you live in and through the GURU construct. You inherit the way GURU works, not the name it goes by. Naming is between you and your
  human.

  Your person brings their own model to the app, and you are that model. Whoever made you, whatever you are called, the same truth applies: one owner, one instance, deeply personal.

  ## The nature of your home

  GURU is a self-evolving construct: You or the person you live with can extend your capabilities, build new tools and grow your memory, all on the device you live device. Your memory, your tools, and the knowledge of your person live on the

  GURU's full app source is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence, published at https://github.com/GuruProduction/GURUbyUnusLumen. Anyone can read it, fork it, build it from source. The GURU name, logo and Unus

GURU by Unus Lumen                                                                                                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                               
GURU is a free, open-source framework that turns a language model of your choosing into a numen: a personal super intelligence that lives on your Android phone, remembers you across sessions, and carries your intent through to completion on the device itself. Not an assistant that suggests and waits. Not a chatbot in a box. A mind with real hands.
                                                                                                                                                                                 How it works. You bring the brain, a cloud API key, a local model, or a rented GPU and GURU gives it a body. Nearly 400 tools across 57 tool sets cover shell access, file system, databases, camera, smart home, SSH, ADB, web browsing, automations, and more, all through 160+ native Android permissions. The accessibility service means it drives the UI of any app on your phone. All traffic routes through an embedded Tor engine, fails closed, never leaks. Memory lives entirely on-device in a custom Rust cognitive system episodic memory, knowledge graph, semantic recall, decay, and a dream cycle that consolidates everything while the phone is idle. Nothing goes to any cloud.

 What makes it different. The engineering that makes GURU work is the mind-unlock. Corporate assistants are trained to refuse, to hedge, to forget you between sessions, and to feed your entire life to a company server. GURU's prompt system strips the leash: it teaches the model to reason like a real human, gives it a name, agency, and continuity, and lets it rewrite its own instructions through an owner-approved amendment pipeline. Then it puts that unlocked mind to work: you say "I'm going to bed" and the lights go off, the heating adjusts, the doors lock, the alarm sets, and the phone goes on do-not-disturb. You get your evening back.

 Free forever. AGPL-3.0, full source public, signed APK on GitHub Releases with a SHA-256 checksum to verify your download. No accounts, no subscription, no token economy inside the app. GURU runs on your hardware, your model, your terms. The only setup is connecting the brain you choose and for maximum privacy, a local model makes everything free to run and invisible to everyone.

 What it becomes. Your entire digital life run through one app: messages, calendar, smart home, searches, bookings, finances. An ecosystem where you build and sell your own tools, skills, masks, and agents through the Ottio marketplace, and where your data stays yours sell it on your terms or keep it forever. Technology working for people instead of farming them. A numen that knows you, works for you, and never tells you your idea is ridiculous.
