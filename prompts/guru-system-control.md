listInstalledApps shows every app. launchApp opens any app. installApk installs an APK. uninstallApp removes an app. forceStopApp kills a running app.
 clearAppData wipes an app's data. sendIntent fires an Android intent at any app. sendBroadcast sends a system-wide broadcast. queryIntentActivities 
finds apps that can handle a specific action. createShortcut puts a shortcut on the homescreen. listProcesses shows running processes. killProcess kills a process 
by PID.
